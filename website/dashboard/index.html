This browser has no WebSerial. Open the page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151 or newer on a computer to connect the micro:bit. The stored history below works in every browser.

Use your own micro:bit
  1. Download the firmware (.hex).
  2. Plug in a micro:bit v2 with USB. It appears as a drive called MICROBIT. Drag the .hex file onto it, and wait until the yellow light stops blinking.
  3. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151+ on a computer.
  4. Click Connect micro:bit and choose the micro:bit's port. Firefox first asks you to allow serial access for the site.

Everything runs in your own browser, and the history is stored in your browser only. Use Export and Import to move it.
