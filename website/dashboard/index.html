This browser has no WebSerial. Open the page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151 or newer on a computer to connect the micro:bit. The stored history below works in every browser.

Set up your micro:bit (one time only)
  1. Plug your micro:bit into this computer with a USB cable. A new drive called MICROBIT appears, like a USB stick.
  2. Download the Hytte program: Download Hytte for micro:bit Tip: if your browser asks where to save, choose the MICROBIT drive and you can skip step 3.
  3. Open your Downloads folder and drag hytte-monitoring.hex onto the MICROBIT drive. The yellow light on the back of the micro:bit blinks. Wait until it stops.
  4. Click Connect micro:bit at the top of this page and choose the micro:bit. The board shows ? and this page shows "Connected".

The program stays on the micro:bit, so you do this only once. Next time, just plug it in and click Connect. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox (version 151 or newer) on a computer.

For advanced users: flash it from a terminal

Paste the command for your system into a terminal (for example VS Code's). It downloads the program from this page and copies it onto the MICROBIT drive (steps 2 and 3 in one).
